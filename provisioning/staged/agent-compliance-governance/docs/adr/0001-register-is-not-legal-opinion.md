# ADR 0001: Register is not a legal opinion

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-02

## Context

Legal status, applicability, effective dates, litigation and rulemaking can change and often depend on entity-specific facts. A public repository cannot establish all relevant facts or substitute for legal advice.

## Decision

The legal register records source-linked monitoring metadata only. Each record includes applicability triggers, uncertainties, review status, source authority, verification date and next review. Entity-specific applicability remains unassessed until qualified counsel reviews the actual facts. Voluntary frameworks, proposed rules and court orders remain distinct from enacted statutes.

## Consequences

- No legal or compliance certification is claimed.
- Proposed control catalogue entries remain proposed.
- The public evidence index is empty and must not contain privileged or personal information.
- Legal review and a private evidence-management process are required before production use.
