# Security Policy

This repository contains public legal-monitoring metadata, proposed control definitions and validators. It is not a legal advice service, compliance certification, evidence vault or authoritative legal record system.

## Reporting

Do not publish exploitable vulnerability details, credentials, personal data, privileged legal advice or confidential evidence in public issues. Use GitHub private vulnerability reporting or a private security advisory only if enabled for this repository. No dedicated private reporting address is published here; the owner must configure an approved private channel if GitHub private reporting is unavailable.

## Data and integrity expectations

- Do not place personal data, sensitive case records or confidential legal advice in the public repository.
- Treat source documents and URLs as untrusted; preserve source authority, retrieval date and integrity metadata.
- Evidence digests prove only that bytes match a digest; they do not prove truth, legal sufficiency or compliance.
- No control may be marked implemented or effective without owner approval and objective evidence.
- No law may be marked applicable without entity-specific legal review.
