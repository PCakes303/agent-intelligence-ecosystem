# Code of Conduct

Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good faith while evaluating evidence, give and receive constructive feedback, and focus criticism on ideas and implementation rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, intimidation, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through a verified channel. Do not include personal or sensitive information in public issues.
